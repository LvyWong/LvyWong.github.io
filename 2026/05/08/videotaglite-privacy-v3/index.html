LvyMediaFactory Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 8, 2026

Welcome to LvyMediaFactory (also known as “Local Video Transcoder”, hereinafter referred to as “this App”). This Privacy Policy is designed to help you understand how we collect, use, store and protect your information, as well as your rights in relation to such information. The operator of this App (hereinafter referred to as “we”) respects your privacy and is committed to strictly complying with major global privacy regulations, including but not limited to the requirements of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the US California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA).

Please read this policy carefully before using the App. Your use of this App means that you agree that we will process your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please stop using this App.

  1. Definitions and Interpretations
    1.1 Key Definitions
    This App: refers to the Android application you are currently using, namely LvyMediaFactory.
    Personal data: refers to any information related to an identified or identifiable natural person (in accordance with the GDPR definition), or “personal information” (in accordance with the CCPA/CPRA definition), including but not limited to device information, video data, etc.
    Video metadata: refers to video technology metadata such as format, codec, resolution, frame rate, duration and other technical data. This data helps us improve video editing and conversion functions. Video metadata does not include the video content itself.
    SDK: refers to the third-party software development kit integrated into this App, whose privacy policy is provided through a separate link (see the “Information Processing of Third-Party SDKs” section below for details).
    Offline processing: refers to data processing operations completed locally on your device.
    You: refers to the natural person user using this App.

    1.2 Sdk Privacy policy

    This is the Third-party SDK official in my apps.
    Firebase Crashlytics: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
    Firebase Analytics: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
    Google AdMob: You can visit the following link to learn how Google processes your data:
    https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

  2. Information Collection and Use
    2.1 Information Collected by This App

    This application may collect some Video metadata about your videos. This data is used to improve our video editing features and performance. We do not collect your video content.

    Apart from the integrated SDK, this application does not upload any information other than the aforementioned metadata to any server and only processes the following information locally on your device:

    2.1.1 Video Access and Local Processing
    Collection Scope: With your explicit authorization (specifically through the system-provided media selector), this application may access videos on your device.

    Permission Acquisition: This application will not request your device’s video/media library.
    Purpose of Use: This app is solely for providing offline video transcoding and offline video play functionality.
    Offline Video Transcoding: Processes and converts video formats, resolutions, or bitrates locally on your device (e.g., video compression, format conversion) to optimize display and storage.

    Offline Video Playback: Plays videos within the app for convenient viewing and management.

    Data Storage: Related video data is not stored locally on your device, nor is it uploaded to any server or third party. You can revoke video access permissions at any time through your device settings.
    2.1.2 Other local data

    Except for videos, the business code of the application will not collect, store or upload any other user information (such as name, contact information, location information, etc.).

    2.2 Information processing of third-party SDKs

    This App integrates third-party SDKs to implement some functions. The SDK may collect and process your information, and its specific rules are independently formulated by the SDK provider. You can view the information processing of the SDK provider through the following links:

    Firebase Crashlytics: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy?hl=en-us#examples-end-user-data-processed-by-firebase

    Firebase Analytics: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy?hl=en-us#examples-end-user-data-processed-by-firebase
    (Note: Firebase Analytics is used to collect anonymized app usage data and device identifiers to help us analyze user behavior and improve the App’s performance. You can opt-out of Analytics data collection through your device settings.)

    Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy

    https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

    (Note: AdMob is used to serve advertisements within the App. It may collect and use device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID - AAID) and usage data to provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements. Firebase Analytics is used to collect anonymized app usage data and device identifiers to help us analyze user behavior and improve the App’s performance. You can opt-out of Analytics data collection and personalized advertising through your device settings.)

    Please read the content in the above links carefully to understand the type of information collected by the SDK and your rights.

  3. Data Retention and Deletion
    3.1 Local Data Retention
    For the information processed and stored locally on your device by this App (such as video output configuration preferences), the retention period is determined by you: you can delete this data at any time through the device settings or the functions in this App.

    3.2 Video Metadata Retention

    Video metadata is collected using the Firebase Analytics SDK, and its lifecycle and rules follow Firebase’s data collection rules. For specific rules, please refer to the “Information Processing of Third-Party SDKs” section and the specific rules of the Firebase Analytics SDK.

    3.3 SDK Data Retention

    The retention rules for information collected by the SDK are specified by its provider. Please refer to the “Information processing of third-party SDKs” for details.(Part 2.2)

  4. Your Rights

    According to GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and other regulations, you have the following rights. The exercise of rights for local data processed by this App itself and data processed by the SDK is different:

    4.1 Rights to Local Data of this App

    Since this App itself does not upload or store your information to the server, you can directly manage the data in the following ways:

    Access and Correction: You have the right to access the personal data stored by the App on your device, and you can correct inaccurate information through device settings or in-app functions (if any).

    Right to Deletion: Clear the cached data of this App or uninstall this App through device settings (such as “App Management”) to delete all locally stored information.
    Right to restrict processing: You can clear the app data to clear the access of the video.
    Right to withdraw consent: You can withdraw your authorization for video access permissions at any time (the path is the same as above). After withdrawing consent, the application will stop the relevant functions, but it will not affect the legal processing that has been completed based on your consent.

    4.2 Rights to SDK data and Video Metadata

    Video metadata is collected using the Firebase Analytics SDK, and its lifecycle and rules follow Firebase’s data collection rules. You can check the Firebase Analytics page for specific rights.

    The information collected by the SDK is subject to the privacy policy of its provider.

    Review the “Information processing of third-party SDKs” (Part 2.2) clause to check the Rights to SDK data of the third-party SDK;

    You can exercise your rights in the following ways:

    Submit a rights request according to the contact information specified by the SDK provider (such as the customer service email address).

    4.3 Supplementary rights under specific regulations
    GDPR related: You have the right to request processing restrictions, data portability (if applicable), and withdraw your consent to SDK information processing at any time (through the guidance in the SDK privacy policy);
    CCPA/CPRA related: You have the right to request deletion of personal information, choose not to sell personal information (if the SDK involves data sales, the specific details are subject to the SDK privacy policy), and we will not discriminate against you based on your exercise of rights;
    Ads Personalization: You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting the “Ad” or “Privacy” settings on your mobile device (e.g., “Opt out of Ads Personalization” or “Reset Advertising ID”).
    CalOPPA related: The link to this privacy policy will be prominently displayed on the “Settings-About” page of this App for your convenience.

  5. Data Security and Cross-Border Transmission
    5.1 Local Data Security

    The application only follows the Android standard data storage method, including:

    Internal storage: used to save application configuration, temporary cache (such as video thumbnails) and other data, which can only be accessed by the application itself.
    External storage: After obtaining your authorization, you can read/write video files in the device’s public folders (following the Android system file access specification).

    5.2 Cross-border transfer (SDK only)

    SDK may transfer the collected information to the country/region where it operates (which may be different from the data protection laws in your region). The SDK provider will ensure that cross-border transfer complies with relevant regulatory requirements. For details, please refer to the “Information processing of third-party SDKs” (Part 2.2) clause in the third-party SDK privacy policy.

  6. Third-party services

    Except for the aforementioned SDK (Firebase and AdMob), the application does not integrate other third-party services, nor will it share, sell or transfer your personal data to any third party. The data collected by AdMob is used for the sole purpose of delivering and analyzing advertisements.

  7. Contact us

    If you have any questions about this privacy policy or need to exercise relevant rights, please contact us through the following methods:

    Email: wonglvy1997hk@gmail.com

    We will respond to your request within 30 working days (in accordance with the time limit requirements of GDPR, CCPA and other regulations).

  8. Update of Privacy Policy

    We may update this privacy policy from time to time. After the update, we will inform you through the notification in this App or other reasonable means, and update the “Last Update Date” at the top of the policy. It is recommended that you check this policy regularly to understand the latest information processing rules.
